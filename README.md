🤖 Negotia AI

AI Agents Negotiate. x402 Enables. Algorand Secures.

🚀 Overview

Negotia AI is an autonomous AI commerce platform where intelligent Buyer and Seller AI agents negotiate deals on behalf of humans. Instead of manually comparing vendors, bargaining, and managing payments, users simply describe what they need, and AI agents handle the negotiation process automatically.

Once both agents reach an agreement, x402 enables machine-to-machine payment requests, while Algorand Smart Contracts securely manage escrow, ensuring that funds are released only after the agreed conditions are fulfilled.

Negotia AI demonstrates the future of Agentic Commerce, where AI agents don't just assist users—they actively participate in secure commercial transactions.

---

❗ Problem Statement

Traditional commerce is slow, manual, and trust-dependent.

Buyers spend significant time:

- Searching for sellers
- Comparing prices
- Negotiating deals
- Managing payments
- Verifying deliveries

Businesses also face challenges with payment disputes, communication barriers, and cross-border transactions.

---

💡 Our Solution

Negotia AI automates the complete commerce workflow.

Instead of humans negotiating:

🤖 Buyer AI represents the customer.

🤖 Seller AI represents the vendor.

Both AI agents negotiate autonomously based on price, quantity, delivery time, and user preferences.

After agreement:

- x402 enables automated payment requests.
- Algorand Smart Contracts create a secure escrow.
- Payment is released only after confirmation.
- Reputation is permanently recorded for future trust.

⚙️ How It Works

1. User logs into Negotia AI.
2. User enters product requirements.
3. Buyer AI searches suitable sellers.
4. Seller AI agents negotiate automatically.
5. Best offer is selected.
6. User reviews and approves the deal.
7. x402 initiates the payment request.
8. Algorand Smart Contract locks funds in escrow.
9. Delivery is confirmed.
10. Payment is released automatically.
11. Reputation scores are updated.
